# Content

- **Language:** ⟨from BRIEF: English · pt-BR⟩ across the interface. In pt-BR write *sua/seu*, never
  *tua/teu*.
- **Terse and objective.** What happened, then what to do. No developer notes, rationale, task ids or
  explanations on the screen; they belong in comments or here.
- **Labels** are sentence case, one or two words, a verb for an action: "Issue", "Re-check", "Copy".
  No trailing period on titles, labels, buttons or badges. Same action, same word, everywhere.
- **Pane heads** use the uppercase `label` style: SOURCE, OUTPUT, ⟨RUN-007⟩. Nothing else does.
- **Ids and paths** exactly as on disk, always mono: ⟨examples from this product⟩.
- **Hints are facts:** a unit, a cost (`+59% size`), a lock (`locked · engine contract`), a destination
  (`→ .history/`). Never a sentence explaining the setting.
- **Engine messages** are shown verbatim; codes are never renamed.
- **Missing values** show `—`, never `0`, `null` or `N/A`.
- **No emoji.** Marks: `iconography.md`.
